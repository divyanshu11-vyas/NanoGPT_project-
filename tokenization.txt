I used a veryfy token distrubuter besause

Total token count → how much training data you actually have.
EOT count → whether your document boundaries/separators survived encoding.
Most common tokens → whether something weird dominates the dataset.
Unique-token count → whether your dataset actually uses a reasonable variety of the tokenizer's vocabulary.
Vocabulary coverage → rough indication of how much of the tokenizer vocabulary your dataset touches.
